MASTER SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master SaaS Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
March 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Aurora Analytics, Inc., a
Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 100 Market Street,
San Francisco, CA 94105 ("Provider") and Northwind Logistics Corporation, a
Washington corporation with its principal place of business at 500 Pike Place,
Seattle, WA 98101 ("Customer").

1. SUBSCRIPTION. Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable
subscription to access the Aurora Analytics Platform ("Service") during the
Term.

2. FEES. Customer shall pay the following annual fees:

   Platform license (up to 50 users):   $36,000.00
   Premium support tier:                 $9,000.00
   Onboarding and data migration:        $5,000.00
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Total Annual Fees:                   $50,000.00

3. TERM AND TERMINATION. The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12)
months from the Effective Date, automatically renewing for successive one-year
terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least
sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term. Either party may
terminate for material breach upon thirty (30) days written notice if the
breach is not cured within such period.

4. INDEMNIFICATION. Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other
party from and against any third-party claims arising out of the indemnifying
party's breach of this Agreement, gross negligence, or willful misconduct.

5. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State
of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-law principles.
