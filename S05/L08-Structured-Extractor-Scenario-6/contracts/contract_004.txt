SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Software Development Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made
effective as of February 12, 2026, between Polaris Engineering Studios LLC,
a California limited liability company ("Studio"), and Greenfield Energy
Cooperative, a Colorado nonprofit cooperative ("Sponsor").

1. SERVICES. Studio will design, build, and deliver the Greenfield Member
Portal, a customer-facing web application for energy account management,
billing visibility, and conservation goal tracking. The full feature
specification is set forth in Exhibit A.

2. COMPENSATION. Sponsor shall compensate Studio according to the following
deliverable schedule:

   Phase 1 — Discovery and design system:        $32,000.00
   Phase 2 — Authentication and account module:  $48,000.00
   Phase 3 — Billing and payment integration:    $62,000.00
   Phase 4 — Conservation tracking module:       $39,000.00
   Phase 5 — QA, accessibility audit, launch:    $24,000.00
                                                 ─────────────
   Total Project Investment:                    $215,000.00

Payment shall be made within thirty (30) days of each phase acceptance.

3. TERM. The Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall
continue until completion of all five phases, anticipated to occur within
approximately twenty-eight (28) weeks.

4. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Sponsor may terminate this Agreement for
convenience upon fifteen (15) days written notice. In such event, Sponsor
shall pay Studio for all work performed through the date of termination,
plus a kill-fee equal to twenty percent (20%) of the remaining unpaid
contract value.

5. INDEMNIFICATION. Studio agrees to indemnify and hold Sponsor harmless
against any third-party claims that the delivered software infringes any
existing copyright, patent, trademark, or trade secret of such third party,
provided that Sponsor notifies Studio promptly of any such claim and grants
Studio sole control over the defense and settlement.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Upon final payment of all amounts due under this
Agreement, Studio assigns to Sponsor all right, title, and interest in the
deliverables, excluding any pre-existing Studio tools, frameworks, or
libraries that may be incorporated into the deliverables.

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State
of Colorado.
