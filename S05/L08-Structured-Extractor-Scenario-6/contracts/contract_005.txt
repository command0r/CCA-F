RESELLER AGREEMENT

This Reseller Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of September 1,
2026 by and between Cascade Networks Equipment Inc., a Washington corporation
("Manufacturer"), and Pacific Rim Distribution Group GmbH, a German limited
liability company ("Reseller").

1. APPOINTMENT. Manufacturer hereby appoints Reseller as its non-exclusive
authorized reseller of the Cascade Networks product line for the territory
consisting of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland
(the "Territory"), and Reseller accepts such appointment subject to the
terms of this Agreement.

2. PRODUCTS AND PRICING. Reseller may purchase Products from Manufacturer
at the wholesale prices set forth in Exhibit B, which Manufacturer may revise
on sixty (60) days written notice. Reseller is free to set its own resale
prices to end customers in the Territory.

3. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT. Reseller commits to a minimum annual purchase
volume of 4,000 units in the first contract year, increasing by ten percent
(10%) in each subsequent year of the Term.

4. TERM AND RENEWAL. The initial Term of this Agreement is three (3) years
from the Effective Date. The Agreement shall automatically renew for
successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of
non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days prior to the end of the
then-current Term.

5. TERMINATION FOR CAUSE. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately
upon written notice if the other party: (a) materially breaches this Agreement
and fails to cure within thirty (30) days; (b) becomes insolvent or files for
bankruptcy; or (c) ceases to conduct business in the ordinary course.

6. INDEMNIFICATION. Manufacturer shall indemnify Reseller against any
third-party claim that an authorized Product infringes a patent, copyright,
or trademark, provided that the alleged infringement is based on the Product
as delivered by Manufacturer and not on modifications made by Reseller or
end customers.

7. EXPORT CONTROL. Reseller acknowledges that the Products may be subject
to U.S. export control laws and regulations and agrees to comply with all
applicable export and re-export controls in conducting its business under
this Agreement.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION. This Agreement shall be governed by the
laws of the State of Washington, excluding its conflict-of-laws principles.
The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal
courts located in King County, Washington.
