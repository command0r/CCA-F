PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered
into effective as of January 8, 2026, between Helios Cloud Partners LLC, a
Texas limited liability company ("Consultant"), and Meridian Health Systems,
Inc., a Massachusetts corporation ("Client").

SCOPE OF SERVICES. Consultant shall provide cloud migration services as
described in Statement of Work #2026-01.

FEES AND PAYMENT. Client shall pay Consultant the following fees, invoiced
monthly:

  Discovery and architecture phase (Weeks 1-4):    $48,000.00
  Migration execution (Weeks 5-16):               $192,000.00
  Post-migration support (Weeks 17-20):            $36,000.00
  Project management (across all phases):          $24,000.00
                                                  ─────────────
  Total Contract Value:                           $300,000.00

TERM. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for
twenty (20) weeks unless extended in writing by both parties.

TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days
written notice. If Client terminates without cause, Client shall pay for all
services performed through the date of termination plus a 10% wind-down fee
calculated on remaining contract value.

INDEMNIFICATION. Consultant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless
Client from any third-party claims arising from Consultant's negligent
performance of the services. This indemnification shall not apply to claims
arising from Client's modifications to Consultant's deliverables.

GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
